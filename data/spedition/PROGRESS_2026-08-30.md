## Classification rules
1. Prefer explicit Austrian entity headcount from WKO/company website.
2. Next use karriere.at employer employee bands, but reject false-positive name matches.
3. Group-level website or LinkedIn evidence is never presented as standalone Austrian-entity headcount; it goes to `B_LIKELY_30_PLUS_GROUP`.
4. `U_NOT_PROVEN` means only that no reliable size proof was found; it does **not** mean the company is small.
5. Companies that merely hold a Spediteur permission but are not a core external freight/logistics provider are separated as `X_NON_CORE` / adjacent rather than mixed into the main outreach list.
